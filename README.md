# NutriQuest : Gamified Nutrition Tracker 

## Plan what you eat before you buy it.

## 💡 The Idea 

The idea came to me while grocery shopping. I started wondering: Wouldn't it be more useful to understand the nutritional value of the food I'm buying before I bring it home? And what if I could also get ideas for meals to cook with those ingredients?

Instead of tracking nutrition only after preparing a meal, NutriQuest aims to help users make more informed grocery choices, understand their nutritional progress, and discover meals they can prepare with the ingredients they buy.
To make the experience more engaging, I added a gamification system where consistently meeting nutrition goals helps users level up and unlock AI-generated meal plans.

## ✨ Key Features

## Grocery-Based Nutrition Tracking

Add grocery items directly to the app and explore their nutritional values before deciding what to buy.

## USDA Nutrition API Integration

Fetch nutritional information through the USDA API .

## Gamified Progression

 The progression system adds a sense of achievement to building healthier habits.

## AI-Powered Meal Planning

Unlock AI-generated meal plans as you progress through the levels, helping turn your grocery list into practical meal ideas.

## Mobile-Friendly Interface

Built with Flet and Python to create an interactive interface while keeping the application development within the Python ecosystem.

## Modular Architecture

The application is designed with extensibility in mind, making it easier to explore additional APIs, machine learning features, and new reward systems.

## What Makes NutriQuest Different?

Most people think about nutrition when they are preparing food or logging what they have already eaten.NutriQuest explores a different starting point: the moment you decide what groceries to buy.

The goal is to connect three steps in one experience:

1. Choose : Enter the groceries you're considering.
2. Understand : Explore their nutritional contribution toward your goals.
3. Plan : Discover meal ideas using the ingredients you've selected.
